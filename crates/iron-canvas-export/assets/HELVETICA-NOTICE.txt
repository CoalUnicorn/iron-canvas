Helvetica advance-width provenance
=================================

The WinAnsi advance tables in src/common/helvetica.rs are derived data.
The conversion selects AFM glyph names through the Adobe Glyph List and
indexes their WX values by WinAnsi byte. NBSP uses space, soft hyphen uses
hyphen, and 0x7f uses bullet. Unassigned and control bytes have zero width.
No font program is embedded. Kerning pairs and glyph bounds are not copied.
All 256 advances were compared across the source faces: Helvetica-Oblique
matches Helvetica, and Helvetica-BoldOblique matches Helvetica-Bold.

Sources:
https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/tree/v3.10.7/lib/matplotlib/mpl-data/fonts/pdfcorefonts
https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-aglfn/blob/master/glyphlist.txt
